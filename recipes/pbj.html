Guide to make the perfect PB&J sandwich

Credit to the OG photo right here

Extremly simple and delicous dish that you can make in a minute with 3 ingredients in less than a minute

Ingredients

  • Peanut butter: crunchy/creamy
  • Jam or jelly: grape/strawberry/raspberry/your prefered flavour
  • Bread: American white bread is prefered, but any bread should work just fine

Steps

  1. Cut your bread into slices, if they aren't already
  2. Spread peanut butter on one face of a slice of bread
  3. spread jelly/jam on one face of another slice of bread
  4. Put both slice of bread together so the face with peanut butter meet the face with jelly/jam
  5. Enjoy your PB&J sandwich
